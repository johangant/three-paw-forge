These are downloaded viewer renditions, up to 720 pixels tall, not original camera files. Source JPEG copies in `assets/source/` are normalised to RGB without EXIF metadata. They are outside the public build. Descriptive captions are based on the visible pieces, not verified material specifications or project histories.

Responsive progressive JPEG files in `public/images/` preserve the full image without cropping or upscaling. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/optimise-images.py` (requires Pillow). The website serves its own local copies and makes no Google Photos requests.

## Contact area map

The static JPEG `public/images/contact-area-map.jpg` is rendered by `scripts/render-contact-map.py` from the bounded OpenStreetMap extract in `assets/maps/contact-area.json`, retrieved on 4 November 2026 from https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-6.385,54.956,-6.345,54.974. Only map geometry and relevant map tags are retained. No tile server is used by the website or rendering script.

Map data copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database Licence (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Map attribution is visible in the JPEG and linked in the page caption. OpenStreetMap's extract labels the road “Drunminning Road”; this spelling is retained in the rendered map.

The map bounds cover the area around postcode BT44 9PP, whose centroid was retrieved from https://api.postcodes.io/postcodes/BT449PP. Number 206 could not be geocoded, so no exact property marker is shown. The address link performs a search rather than claiming an exact location.
