I wanted to see if I could fire weld steel in my home forge, by hand, and turns out I just about can. This little die is surprisingly heavy for its size and I learned a lot from making it.

I had some high carbon steel offcuts from a knife project which I combined with lower carbon steel. I welded these into a stack, got it nice and hot, hammered it together then cut in half and repeated a few times.

Once cooled I shaped it and dipped it into instant coffee which etched the pattern and brought it to life.

Quite tricky in my small gas forge but with some borax and some patience this turned out nicely. Next time I'm going to add some twists to give it a bit more life and movement.
