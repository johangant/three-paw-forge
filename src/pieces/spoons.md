These copper and steel coffee scoop/serving spoons are popular and look right at home in any country style kitchen or trendy cafe. Use them with dry ingredients or just as a decorative piece.

They're fun to make, have a reassuring weight in the hand and there are many design variants I've made. My first one was made from old boiler copper sheet and offcuts from other projects and it's still the one I use at home every day for my morning brew. I expect it will outlive me!

These utensils change over time with the copper and steel developing their own natural patina depending on how they're used and where they're kept. They're easy to look after and refresh if you like the bright polished copper look, or the deeper developed patina of an established and integral part of your kitchen.

Combined with a gift box and fire engraved message (pyrography) these make for an amazingly unique gift for someone special.
