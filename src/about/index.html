About the maker

Hello, I’m Johan.
The hands behind
Three Paw Forge.

Three Paw Forge was named after the resident cat, Patch, who lost a leg after a disagreement with a passing BMW. Usually found observing from a safe distance, passing silent judgement on my efforts and quietly hoping for a brush or snack to be brought her way.

I'm a software developer by trade and working with metal in a creative way gives me a counterbalance to digital life by both producing tangible, physical things but also refining my techniques and understanding of materials and tools. This site is a small collection of that work.

I’ll be sharing finished pieces alongside the process: the experiments, the details, and what I learn as I go.

More from the workshop coming soon.

Useful objects and decorative details

The collection brings together everyday pieces, like a spoon and a door handle, with decorative work such as the horseshoe Christmas tree. Twists, curls and the texture of the metal give each piece its character.
